Local testing 🧪

Download the latest papiro version from the official github repository. and enter its directory.

cd ./papiro

Then preview any folder of markdown documents, or an Obsidian vault:

./bin/preview.sh ~/notes/my-vault

The script builds papiro, indexes your documents (Obsidian vaults are detected by their .obsidian folder) and serves the wiki locally. It only needs python3, and go on systems other than linux x86-64, where it builds esbuild and the papiro indexer the first time.

Once done, the web will be launched in the following URL.

http://127.0.0.1:8000

Run the script again after changing your documents, or pass another port as the second argument.

Congratulations 🎉🎉
— your wiki is now ready to visit in the local environment. —
